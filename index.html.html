Our Story

Rooted in Mumbai,
Born from Tradition

"Every dish tells a story of tradition, love, and the rich flavours of India."

Sandeep Pure Veg has been a cornerstone of Kandivali East since its founding. What began as a humble family kitchen has grown into one of Mumbai's most beloved vegetarian restaurants — a place where every dish carries the warmth of home and the vibrancy of India's diverse culinary traditions.

1994
Sandeep Pure Veg opens its doors in Kandivali East
2003
Expanded kitchen with full North Indian & Chinese menu
2014
Added spacious family dining hall — 100+ seating capacity
2023
Complete interior renovation — luxury ambience upgrade
2026
